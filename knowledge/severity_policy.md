# Severity Policy

## Critical

Use when an event indicates a potentially severe security impact, such as:
- confirmed or strongly indicated compromise of a critical business asset
- active malware affecting a critical service
- confirmed credential compromise involving privileged access
- significant security activity against a business-critical service

## High

Use when there is credible evidence of malicious activity with meaningful risk to an important user, endpoint, server, application, or service.

## Medium

Use when suspicious or policy-violating activity requires investigation but there is no current evidence of major compromise.

## Low

Use for informational, routine, or low-impact security events.

## Analyst rule

Severity should be based on available evidence, affected asset criticality, threat context, and potential business impact. The final response decision remains with an authorized human.
