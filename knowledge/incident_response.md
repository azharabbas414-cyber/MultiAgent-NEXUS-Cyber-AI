# Incident Response Knowledge

## Purpose

This document provides the initial local knowledge base for NEXUS Cyber AI incident investigation.

## Investigation sequence

1. Identify the event and incident ID.
2. Establish the timeline.
3. Identify affected users, assets, IP addresses, domains, hashes, and services.
4. Correlate related security events.
5. Determine severity using the severity policy.
6. Check threat intelligence context for indicators.
7. Assess business impact and affected services.
8. Prepare recommended response actions.
9. Request human approval for any containment or remediation action.
10. Record the investigation and decision in the audit trail.

## Evidence principles

- Prefer correlated evidence over a single isolated event.
- Preserve timestamps and event identifiers.
- Distinguish observed facts from analyst conclusions.
- Treat synthetic data as demonstration data only.
- Do not perform irreversible response actions automatically.
