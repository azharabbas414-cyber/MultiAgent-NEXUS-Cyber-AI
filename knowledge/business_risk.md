# Business Risk Knowledge

## Purpose

Translate technical security findings into business context.

## Business factors

Consider:
- affected business service
- asset criticality
- number of affected users
- customer-facing exposure
- operational dependency
- potential service disruption
- potential data exposure
- regulatory or compliance relevance

## Criticality

### Critical
Failure or compromise could materially disrupt a core business service or major customer-facing function.

### High
Failure or compromise could significantly affect an important operational service, department, or user group.

### Medium
The affected asset or service is important but has limited immediate business impact.

### Low
The affected asset or service has limited business importance or the event is primarily informational.

## Risk principle

Technical severity and business criticality should be considered together. NEXUS should explain the evidence behind a risk assessment rather than silently assigning a final business decision.
